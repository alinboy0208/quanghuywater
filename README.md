# Quang Huy Water — Company Landing Page

> **Note:** This is my very first frontend web development project, built to showcase a clean, responsive company profile and product introduction website for **Quang Huy Water**.

## Overview

A static frontend landing page designed to present **Quang Huy Water**'s bottled water products, quality commitments, and customer ordering/contact information in a straightforward layout.

## Key Sections

- **Hero & Brand Introduction:** Highlights company mission, clean water standards, and core services.
- **Product Showcase:** Displays available bottled water sizes and packaging options for households and offices.
- **Why Choose Us:** Outlines filtration standards, delivery reliability, and customer support.
- **Contact & Quick Order:** Provides company address, hotline, and inquiry details.
